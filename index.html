scannaclips is a local video editing workspace. Organize source videos, trim moments, add captions and brand overlays, and export clips for your review.

View project on GitHub

Make the cut

Choose a clip range and export vertical, square or landscape video from your browser.

Add your style

Upload a custom brand design and add captions to your clip.

Keep it together

Save campaign requirements and manually record clip performance and confirmed earnings.

Runs locally. You control publishing.

The editor runs on your computer. This website provides information about the project and its policies; it does not host the editor or accept video uploads.

TikTok API delivery and automatic Whop syncing are not connected in the current version. Prepared MP4 files stay local until you choose to upload them through a posting platform.

scannaclips is an independent project maintained by scannatarecords. It is not affiliated with TikTok, YouTube or Whop.
