Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

This policy describes the current scannaclips information website and local editing software maintained by scannatarecords. It reflects the current version: a Sandbox TikTok login connection is available, while draft delivery and automatic Whop syncing remain unimplemented.

The public website

These static pages do not contain analytics scripts, advertising or video uploads. They do not set application cookies. The TikTok callback page uses a small script to display a temporary authorization response for you to copy into the local app. It removes the response from the browser address bar and sends no API requests itself. GitHub may receive the initial callback URL as part of hosting the page. GitHub hosts the site through GitHub Pages and may process request information such as IP addresses and browser details under its Privacy Statement. Following external links opens services with their own privacy practices.

Information in the local app

The local app processes the video files and links, imported source metadata, overlay images, captions, pasted comments, clip ranges, campaign requirements, post links and performance or earnings figures you provide. It uses this information to perform imports, edits, exports and library organization at your request.

In the local version, uploaded media and library records are stored on your computer in the app's local storage and database directories. YouTube imports and prepared MP4 drafts are stored in the app's .scannaclips directory; local database and uploaded-media storage is kept under .wrangler. Rendering happens in your browser. Local sign-in uses a development session cookie to control workspace access; it does not connect your TikTok or Whop account.

Connections to other services

Importing a YouTube link contacts YouTube through the local downloader. Media-tool setup downloads tools from their distribution providers, including GitHub; the downloader may also retrieve runtime components from GitHub. Those services receive the network requests needed for these actions. scannaclips does not extract browser passwords, cookies or account sessions for these imports.

TikTok and Whop operate independently. When you open their websites and upload a video or submit information there, those services handle that data under their own policies. scannaclips currently stores prepared TikTok files locally and manually entered Whop metrics; it does not automatically send them to either service. If you choose to connect the TikTok Sandbox app, scannaclips sends your authorization code and app credentials to TikTok to obtain account tokens. It requests basic profile access and draft-upload permission, and stores the returned account identifier, display name and tokens locally to maintain that connection. On Windows these credentials are encrypted for your Windows account; on other systems they are stored in a restricted local file. Disconnect revokes TikTok access and removes the local credentials. The current version does not send draft videos through the API.

Sharing and retention

The current local app does not send your library to the maintainer or use it for advertising. Files and records remain on your computer until you remove them. Deleting the software alone may leave data and exports behind. Backups, downloaded exports and copies uploaded to other platforms remain separately until you delete them there.

Your choices and security

You can stop using the app, edit manually recorded information and remove locally stored files and records. Back up anything you want to keep before removing storage directories. Protect your device and do not expose the local development server to the public internet. If you supply information in a support request, share only what is needed and avoid public posts containing credentials or sensitive content.

Privacy requests and contact

For privacy questions about scannaclips, contact the maintainer through the GitHub repository or contact information on the maintainer's profile. Request a private contact channel before sharing sensitive details. For data held by GitHub, TikTok, YouTube or Whop, use that provider's privacy and account controls. The local app's data is held on your own device, so the maintainer cannot access or delete it remotely.

Changes

Material changes to data collection, hosting or account connections will be reflected on this page with an updated date. This policy describes the local release and static website, not a future hosted editing service.
